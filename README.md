Public download copies of the 30 public SEXA corpus papers (32 volumes) published by U.F.S. Publishing (Unified Field Systems LLC), author Jered E. McClain with co-authors as printed on each byline.

Every page of every PDF here, the front cover included, carries:

- the U.F.S. seal on the left,
- the DOI seal on the right ("DOI: NOT YET ASSIGNED" where no DOI is on record),
- one Oblivion Mark at no more than 4% opacity,

and each file is sealed with CORNYCoded (fingerprint `CC-41D4E54A1CA3932D`).

The PDFs are release assets: see **Releases → sealed-4pct-2026-11-23**.
Download pattern: `https://github.com/SpaceTimeManhattan/sexa-corpus-sealed/releases/download/sealed-4pct-2026-11-23/<file>`

Catalog numbers (W-numbers) are permanent and are never renumbered. Papers under peer review are not included here.
